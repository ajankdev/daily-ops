### How I cut our GCP bill by $4,200/mo in one afternoon — commands included
**Source:** r/GoogleCloud

> **Category:** [GCP_K8S_CORE]
> **Summary:** The author, a member of a small engineering team, reduced their GCP bill by $4,200/mo by identifying and eliminating unused resources, including persistent disks, stopped compute instances, orphaned snapshots, static IPs with no attachment, and load balancers with zero traffic. They used various `gcloud` commands to detect and delete these unused resources.
> **Impact:** The optimizations resulted in significant cost savings, with the largest savings coming from rightsizing Cloud SQL instances ($2,460/mo) and eliminating unused resources such as stopped instances ($890/mo) and unattached disks ($680/mo). The author's approach demonstrates the importance of regular audits and resource optimization in managing GCP costs.
