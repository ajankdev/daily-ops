### A cheap model did not help because the agent kept calling the same tool five times
**Source:** r/GoogleCloud

> **Category:** [AI_MODELS]
> **Summary:** The post discusses an experiment where a cheaper model was used in an internal research agent, but it did not significantly reduce costs. Upon further analysis, it was found that the agent was making repeated calls to the same tool, which was causing the high costs. The team used Braintrust to analyze token counts, latency, cost, and tool-call patterns, and implemented loop detection and deduplication of tool outputs. This led to a reduction in repeated calls and improved latency, with the cheaper model occasionally making the issue worse.
> **Impact:** The experiment highlights the importance of considering the entire workflow and tool-call patterns when evaluating the cost of AI models, rather than just looking at per-call metrics. It also shows that optimizing the agent's behavior and reducing retries can have a greater impact on cost savings than simply switching to a cheaper model.
