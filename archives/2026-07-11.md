### Contributing to U.K. financial sector resilience as a critical third party
**Source:** Google Cloud (General)

> **Category:** [GCP_K8S_CORE]
> **Summary:** Google Cloud has been designated as a critical third party (CTP) to the U.K. financial sector, which will be overseen by the Bank of England, the Prudential Regulation Authority (PRA), and the Financial Conduct Authority (FCA). This designation aims to build sector-wide operational resilience, and Google Cloud will continue to support customers with requirements for operational resilience, outsourcing, and third-party risk management.
> **Impact:** This designation may lead to increased operational requirements and oversight for Google Cloud, potentially impacting the design and operation of GKE, Anthos, and other GCP services used by U.K. financial sector customers, but it demonstrates Google Cloud's commitment to providing a secure, scalable, and resilient platform for digital transformation.

### Why Gemini API doesn't check IAM roles?
**Source:** r/GoogleCloud

> **Category:** [AI_MODELS]
> **Summary:** The Gemini API does not enforce IAM roles, allowing a service account with limited roles (e.g., Logs Writer) to make calls to the generativelanguage.googleapis.com API and incur costs. This is in contrast to Vertex AI, which does enforce IAM permissions. The user is seeking clarification on the intended design and best practices to prevent unintended spend.
> **Impact:** The lack of IAM enforcement on the Gemini API can lead to unintended costs and security risks, as service accounts with limited roles can still make calls to the API and incur charges. This highlights the need for additional controls, such as Org Policy, quotas, or alternative authentication mechanisms, to prevent similar incidents.
