### Accelerating automotive innovation with C4A-metal and Panasonic Automotive vSkipGen
**Source:** Google Cloud (General)

> **Category:** [GCP_K8S_CORE]
> **Summary:** The article discusses the integration of Panasonic Automotive's vSkipGen platform with Google Cloud's C4A-metal, a bare-metal offering. vSkipGen is a next-generation CDC virtualization platform that enables the development, testing, and validation of cockpit software in a flexible, hardware-independent environment. C4A-metal provides a high-performance infrastructure with 96 vCPUs, up to 100Gbps of networking bandwidth, and support for Google Cloud Hyperdisk.
