### A new standard for research: How UC Riverside is securing the path to federal grants with Google Public Sector
**Source:** Google Cloud (General)

> **Category:** [GCP_K8S_CORE]
> **Summary:** The University of California, Riverside (UCR) partnered with Google Public Sector to develop a secure enclave for sensitive research data using Google Cloud's accredited cloud services and Zero Trust architecture. The solution, powered by Stellar Engine, automates and enforces complex security postures, shifting the technical burden away from researchers and into the infrastructure. This enables UCR to confidently bid on and host projects that require high-security controls, such as NIST 800-171 and CMMC Level 2.
> **Impact:** The partnership has significantly improved UCR's research capacity, allowing researchers to focus on making discoveries rather than being bogged down by IT hurdles. The secure enclave provides a hardened environment for sensitive data, ensuring data sovereignty and research agility. This has empowered faculty, facilitated progress in fields that impact public health and national security, and positioned UCR as a top destination for global talent.
