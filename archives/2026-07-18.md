### We deployed a support agent on Vertex AI Agent Engine (ADK) — notes on what worked, including choosing it over our own GKE cluster
**Source:** r/GoogleCloud

> **Category:** [GCP_K8S_CORE]
> **Summary:** The author describes their experience deploying a support agent on Vertex AI Agent Engine (ADK) instead of using their own GKE cluster. They highlight the simplicity of the deployment process using Agent Engine, which eliminated the need for Dockerfiles, Helm charts, and manual rollouts. The author also shares lessons learned, such as the lack of an end-of-session hook, the importance of setting the GOOGLE_CLOUD_LOCATION environment variable, and the use of a learning loop to capture human support replies and improve the agent's memory.
> **Impact:** The use of Vertex AI Agent Engine simplifies the deployment and management of AI-powered support agents, reducing the operational burden and allowing for more focus on developing the agent's capabilities. The lessons learned and shared by the author can help others avoid common pitfalls and improve their own deployments on Agent Engine.
