### Minimize idle accelerators: Native RL job interleaving with co-operative time-slicing in llm-d
**Source:** GKE & Containers

> **Category:** [AI_INFRA]
> **Summary:** The article discusses a solution to minimize idle accelerators in Reinforcement Learning (RL) workloads using co-operative time-slicing through the llm-d project. This approach allows for dynamic interleaving of independent RL jobs onto shared physical hardware, increasing aggregate accelerator duty cycles and improving price-performance. The solution consists of a time-slice orchestrator, snapshot agent, and client libraries, which work together to manage accelerator access and context switching.
> **Impact:** The introduction of co-operative time-slicing has the potential to significantly reduce wasted compute resources and improve the efficiency of RL workloads, leading to cost savings and increased productivity for researchers and engineers working with large language models and RL algorithms.
