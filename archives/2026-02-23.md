### The Hidden Risk of GCP Viewer Role: Cross-Project Disk Replication
**Source:** r/GoogleCloud

> **Category:** [GCP_K8S_CORE]
> **Summary:** The `roles/viewer` role in GCP IAM grants the `compute.disks.useReadOnly` permission, which can be used to clone disks across projects, remove CMEK encryption, and exfiltrate data without requiring KMS access. A workaround via snapshots has also been discovered.
> **Impact:** This vulnerability can have significant operational security implications, as it allows unauthorized access to sensitive data. It is recommended to stop using basic roles and to treat `compute.disks.useReadOnly` as a high-risk permission, similar to being able to download an entire hard drive.
