### PQC in Plaintext: Google Cloud’s post-quantum cryptography roadmap
**Source:** Google Cloud (General)

> **Category:** [GCP_K8S_CORE]
> **Summary:** Google Cloud has outlined its roadmap for migrating to post-quantum cryptography (PQC) by 2029, focusing on three key domains: mitigating Store Now Decrypt Later (SNDL) risks, ensuring integrity against forgery, and enhancing foundational capabilities for cryptographic agility. The roadmap includes enabling quantum-safe key exchange, load balancers, and certificate experimentation, as well as transitioning internal infrastructure and customer-facing services to PQC algorithms.
> **Impact:** The migration to PQC will impact Google Cloud's infrastructure and services, requiring updates to client-side software, management of asymmetric keys, and configuration changes to ensure quantum-safe settings and policies. Customers will need to inventory their cryptographic resources, update their development and Site Reliability Engineering teams, and validate their workflows to ensure a smooth transition.

### Expanding connectivity in the Americas: Introducing Alisios, Canoa, and OlaLuz subsea cables
**Source:** Google Cloud (General)

> **Category:** [GCP_K8S_CORE]
> **Summary:** Google is expanding its global network infrastructure in the Americas with three new subsea cable systems: Alisios, Canoa, and OlaLuz. These cables will connect various countries in the region, including the Dominican Republic, Panama, Chile, and Bermuda, to Google Cloud regions, enhancing connectivity, reliability, and resilience.
> **Impact:** The new subsea cables will improve the reach and reliability of Google Cloud services, enabling faster and more secure data transfer between the Americas and other regions, which can lead to better performance and reduced latency for applications deployed on Google Kubernetes Engine (GKE) and other Google Cloud services.
