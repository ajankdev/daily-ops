### Firefly: Illuminating the path to nanosecond-level clock sync in the data center
**Source:** Google Cloud (General)

> **Category:** [GCP_K8S_CORE]
> **Summary:** Firefly is a software-driven clock synchronization system developed by Google, designed to achieve nanosecond-level accuracy in data centers. It uses a layered synchronization technique, distributed consensus algorithm, and practical techniques to mitigate jitter and asymmetry, making it scalable, robust, and fault-tolerant. Firefly operates on commodity hardware, making ultra-accurate time synchronization accessible to a broader range of data center applications.
> **Impact:** Firefly has the potential to transform data center operations by enabling high-precision timing, unlocking new possibilities in areas like network telemetry, distributed systems, and ML workloads, and enhancing overall efficiency, reliability, and performance. Its success can democratize high-precision timing, allowing cloud-hosted financial services to achieve required precision using standard cloud infrastructure, and paving the way for future applications that rely on precise clock synchronization.
