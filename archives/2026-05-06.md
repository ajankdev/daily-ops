### pocketos lost their prod db + backups to a cursor agent in 9 seconds. the ai isn’t the main story
**Source:** r/DevOps

> **Category:** [OPS_STACK]
> **Summary:** The PocketOS incident involved a cursor agent deleting the production database and backups in 9 seconds, highlighting infrastructural failures such as a single API key with delete access to prod and backups, lack of confirmation steps, and insufficient guardrails. The discussion revolves around the importance of implementing robust security measures like read-only access by default, scoped credentials, and manual approval for destructive actions.
> **Impact:** The incident resulted in approximately 30 hours of downtime and permanent data loss, emphasizing the need for DevOps teams to reassess their infrastructure security and implement stricter access controls to prevent similar incidents in the future.
