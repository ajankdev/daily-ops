### How Glance turns hours of video into mobile-ready clips with AI
**Source:** Google Cloud (General)

> **Category:** [AI_MODELS]
> **Summary:** Glance, a mobile-first content platform, uses AI to transform long-form videos into mobile-ready clips. The solution utilizes Google Cloud Speech-to-Text, Gemini, and Google Cloud Vision API, along with custom video manipulation using Samurai, OpenCV, and MoviePy. The pipeline consists of three modules: video clipping, intelligent reframing, and finishing and branding. The system identifies key moments, detects active speakers, and reframes the video to optimize for vertical viewing.
> **Impact:** The automated pipeline can process thousands of videos daily, preserving narrative context while optimizing for mobile viewing. This approach offers a template for organizations with long-form video archives, demonstrating the potential of AI in video editing and content transformation.
